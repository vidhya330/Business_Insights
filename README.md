📊 Business Insights Dashboard | Power BI

📌 Project Overview

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes sales, profitability, customers, products, regional performance, market share, and forecast accuracy.

The dashboard helps businesses understand performance, identify problems, discover growth opportunities, and make data-driven decisions.

🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze sales and profitability.
- Identify valuable customers and products.
- Compare regional and market performance.
- Monitor market share and revenue contribution.
- Evaluate sales forecast accuracy.
- Identify opportunities to improve profitability.

🚨 Business Problems & Insights

1. Sales vs. Profitability

Problem: High sales do not always mean high profits.

Dashboard Insight:

- Net Sales: ₹3.62 billion
- Gross Margin: 38.32%
- Net Profit Margin: -13.88%

Recommendation: Investigate operating expenses and indirect costs to improve profitability.

2. Customer Performance

Problem: Businesses may not know which customers generate the most value.

Dashboard Insight: Customer revenue and margin analysis helps identify high-value customers and low-margin accounts.

Recommendation: Focus on valuable customer relationships and improve the profitability of low-margin accounts.

3. Product Performance

Problem: High-selling products may not generate the best profits.

Dashboard Insight: Storage, Notebook, and Peripheral categories are major revenue contributors.

Recommendation: Compare product sales, margins, and costs to identify profitable products.

4. Regional Performance

Problem: Sales and profitability differ across regions.

Dashboard Insight: North America (NA) is the leading revenue-generating region, while APAC also contributes significantly.

Recommendation: Strengthen leading markets and investigate performance gaps in other regions.

5. Market Share

Problem: Businesses need to understand their market position and revenue contribution.

Dashboard Insight: Market share trends and division- and channel-level analysis help identify leading business segments.

Recommendation: Use these insights to support pricing, product positioning, and growth strategies.

6. Forecast Accuracy

Problem: Inaccurate forecasts can cause inventory and planning issues.

Dashboard Insight: Forecast Accuracy %, Net Error %, and Absolute Error help identify differences between forecasted and actual sales.

Recommendation: Investigate forecasting errors to improve demand planning and resource allocation.

7. Cost Analysis

Problem: Positive gross margins do not always translate into net profit.

Dashboard Insight: The gap between Gross Margin (38.32%) and Net Profit Margin (-13.88%) highlights the need to investigate business expenses.

Recommendation: Analyze operating costs and improve operational efficiency.

📈 Key Executive Insights

- Revenue: ₹3.62 billion in net sales.
- Profitability: 38.32% gross margin but -13.88% net profit margin.
- Products: Storage, Notebook, and Peripheral are major revenue contributors.
- Region: North America leads in revenue.
- Customers: Customer-level analysis supports better account prioritization.
- Forecasting: Error metrics help identify planning gaps.
- Priority: Improve profitability while maintaining sales performance.
